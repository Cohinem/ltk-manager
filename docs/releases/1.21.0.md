## Material Editor

- `StaticMaterialDef` objects open with a **Material preview** next to the inspector. The preview
  draws the skin that uses the material, or a sphere, cube, plane or cylinder with **Draw on a
  shape**
- **Samplers**, **Params**, **Switches** and **Macros** are tables listing every row the shader
  declares. A row the material does not set shows the shader default, and editing it adds the entry
- Drag a parameter's label to scrub its value while the preview updates, and `Color` / `Tint`
  parameters get a colour picker
- Rows warn about a texture that is not in the game or the project, a parameter the shader does not
  declare, and a switch that rebuilds the shader
- The skin preview has a **Material** pane: click a submesh on the character to edit its material
  next to it
- Materials show as live sphere thumbnails in the Objects grid

## Previews

- **Game shaders** toggle in the skin and map previews draws materials with the game's own shaders,
  including lightmaps and the map sun. It is off by default
- **View mode** menu in the skin, map and particle previews: **Lit**, **Unshaded**,
  **Untextured** and **Wireframe**, with a **Wireframe overlay** option. The choice applies to every
  preview and is remembered
- **Sky** toggle in the map preview and the skin preview's backdrop
- **Hide viewport UI** in the skin preview hides overlays and the gizmo. Press
  Esc to bring them back
- **Bake tangents** in the skin preview writes tangents into the skin's mesh, for PBR authoring with
  RMA textures and normal maps. The mesh must be in the same project layer as the skin
- Previews and the content browser keep their state when you close, maximize or move panes, instead
  of reloading

## Map Preview and Backdrop

- **Sun** popover sets azimuth, elevation, sun, sky and ground colour and strength, starting from
  the map's own sun
- **Post effects** popover edits depth fog, height fog, depth of field and ambient occlusion,
  starting from the map's own post effects
- **Visibility layers** menu picks which of a map's layer variants draw, with a triangle count per
  layer. Layers are numbered, not named, and reset when you pick another map

## Particle Editor

Particle files in a project can now be edited from the emitter inspector, and the preview follows
every change live.

- Emitter properties have readable names such as **Emission Rate** and **Color over Lifetime**, new
  **Initial Motion** and **Motion Over Life** groups, and a **Search emitter properties** box
- Unset properties show their default, and editing one adds it to the file
- **Forces** section adds acceleration, drag, noise, orbital and attraction forces, each with mute,
  solo, a viewport gizmo and a curve
- **Move emitter** and **Rotate emitter** gizmos in the viewport
- Any value can switch between a constant and a curve. Curves take added, deleted, dragged and
  box-selected keys, and gradient keys take a colour
- Number fields step with the arrow keys: Ctrl for fine steps, Shift for coarse steps
- Mesh particles play their skeleton's animation, and a **Character host** picker lets emitters that
  spawn from a character's mesh emit from an animated skin
- Static custom materials preview with their texture, blend and render state. Shader graphs and
  material drivers are not previewed

## Objects Browser

- **Grid** view next to **Tree**, with tile size and thumbnails in **Sort and view**
- Skins show a textured still and particles play when hovered
- Folders open in place with breadcrumbs, and objects with children can be browsed

## Updates

- An update now downloads in the background and installs when you quit LTK Manager, or right away
  with **Restart to Update**
- The app checks for updates again every 4 hours and when the window comes back after an hour
- **Check for Updates** in **Settings > About**, and an **Update to** entry in the tray menu
- Updating is disabled while League is running

### Changed

- Declarations in `game_data.yaml` write a struct as a tag, for example
  `skinMeshProperties: !embed(SkinMeshDataProperties)` with its fields directly under it. A
  hand-written file using the old `!pointer {class: ..., set: ...}` tag form must be rewritten.
  The `embed: {class, set}` mapping form still loads
- **Copy as declaration** keeps fields without a known name, spelled by their `0x` hash, instead of
  leaving them out
- Declarations can create and remove whole objects through an `objects` binding. There is no UI for
  this yet, only hand-written declarations

### Fixed

- Installing a Fantome mod whose hashtable files start with a UTF-8 BOM keeps the hashed names
- Some TFT boards drew almost empty in the map preview
- Foliage and grass on the map backdrop no longer fade out with distance or show dark edges
